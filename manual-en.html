BAKUMATSU BATTLE ON THE SHOGI BOARD

Shinsengumi Shogi — Game Manual

If you already know standard shogi, you can play immediately. The special mode adds unpredictable historical-fiction events and dramatic effects.

Back to Game

1. Your First Match

  1. Select Play vs AI (Beginner is a good starting point) or Two Players.
  2. Press the start button, then tap one of your pieces.
  3. Tap a highlighted destination to move. Captured pieces normally enter your hand.
  4. You can drop a piece from your hand onto an eligible empty square.

Regular matches use a 9 × 9 board. Select Standard Shogi if you want to play without special events.

2. Event Cards

In regular Shinsengumi Shogi matches, each side receives an event card every four of its own turns. Tap to reveal it, then tap again to activate its effect. Revealing a card does not consume a move.

  • EarthquakeThe board shakes. Pieces may move; some squares become temporary holes that cannot be entered.
  • DesertionA soldier may tremble and flee the board, disappearing without joining either player's captured pieces.
  • PurgeA soldier is removed from the board. A dramatic ink-and-blood effect appears.
  • ReinforcementsAn enemy soldier changes allegiance and joins your hand.
  • No IncidentNothing unusual happens; the match continues.

These event effects are creative gameplay inspired by the Bakumatsu era, not literal historical reconstructions.

3. Hitokiri and Your Lord

HitokiriEach individual character tracks their own captures. On that character's third capture, Hitokiri awakens. Their next capture kills the opposing piece, so the piece is lost instead of joining your hand. The character then resets to zero captures. Other characters' captures do not affect this counter.

Abandon the KingIn special mode, you may intentionally make a move that fails to escape check. A warning appears. If your lord is then captured, you lose. This is an invented rule; it is disabled in Standard Shogi.

4. Collection and Saves

Capturing an opponent's character can unlock their collectible portrait and biography. The collection now contains 104 people. Progress is stored in your current browser.

Saving: Story Mode includes a save/resume feature. The current regular-match screen does not provide an in-progress save/resume control. Collection progress and match progress are stored separately.

5. Modes and Controls

  • Play vs AI — beginner, intermediate, or advanced strength.
  • Two Players — alternate turns on one device.
  • Shinsengumi Shogi — recommended mode with the special rules on.
  • Custom Rules — enable or disable specific events and Hitokiri.
  • Standard Shogi — no special events or abandonment of the king.

Fullscreen availability depends on your phone and browser.

6. Story Mode

Play through four chapters in order. Board sizes and special events differ by chapter.

  • Ikedaya Incident: 5 × 6 board.
  • Kinmon Incident: 9 × 9 board.
  • Aburanokoji Incident: 5 × 6 board.
  • Battle of Toba–Fushimi: 9 × 9 board; both sides can use cannon and rifle once each.

Reinforcements can bring pieces into play or return departed soldiers to your hand. Cannon strikes two opposing squares and temporarily blocks impact squares for three turns. Rifle removes the first piece hit along its line; it may miss.
