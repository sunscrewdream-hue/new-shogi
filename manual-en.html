Shinsengumi Shogi — Game Manual

If you already know standard shogi, you can play immediately. The special mode adds unpredictable historical-fiction events and dramatic effects.

1. Standard Shogi Rules

The board has 9 × 9 squares. Each player uses traditional shogi pieces. Captured pieces join your hand and can be dropped back onto the board. Promotion and the familiar movement rules apply. Choose Standard Shogi to switch off all special events.

2. Event Cards

In Shinsengumi Shogi mode, event cards appear at regular turn intervals. Reveal a card and see what fate has in store.

  • EarthquakeThe board shakes. Pieces may move; some squares become temporary holes that cannot be entered.
  • DesertionA soldier may tremble and flee the board, disappearing without joining either player's captured pieces.
  • PurgeA soldier is removed from the board. A dramatic ink-and-blood effect appears.
  • ReinforcementsAn enemy soldier changes allegiance and joins your hand.
  • No IncidentNothing unusual happens; the match continues.

These event effects are creative gameplay inspired by the Bakumatsu era, not literal historical reconstructions.

3. Hitokiri and Your Lord

HitokiriEach individual character tracks their own captures. On that character's third capture, Hitokiri awakens. Their next capture kills the opposing piece, so the piece is lost instead of joining your hand. The character then resets to zero captures. Other characters' captures do not affect this counter.

Abandon the KingIn special mode, you may intentionally make a move that fails to escape check. A warning appears. If your lord is then captured, you lose. This is an invented rule; it is disabled in Standard Shogi.

4. Collection and Saves

Capturing an opponent's character unlocks their collectible portrait and historical biography. Progress is stored on this device in this browser. The collection page offers a separate reset button.

Matches autosave after completed turns. You can also choose Save Game. Use Continue or Load Saved Match on the title screen to resume.

5. Modes and Controls

  • Play vs AI — beginner, intermediate, or advanced strength.
  • Two Players — take turns on a single phone, facing each other.
  • Shinsengumi Shogi — all historical-fiction gameplay features enabled by default.
  • Custom Rules — turn event types and Hitokiri on or off individually.
  • Standard Shogi — play without special events.

Choose JP / English to switch interface text. Pieces and illustrated Japanese inscriptions remain unchanged. Fullscreen availability depends on your phone and browser.
